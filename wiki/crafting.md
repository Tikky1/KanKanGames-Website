# Crafting

Most items in Deepbound are made with **recipes**. A recipe consumes specific ingredients at a specific **station** and gives you a new item.

> 🔒 = Full version only. These recipes do not exist in the demo.

---

## How to craft

1. **Hand crafting:** press `C` to open the crafting panel. Recipes that don't need a station appear here.
2. **Station crafting:** place a Workbench, Furnace or Alchemy Table in the world and **right-click** it (Secondary Use). The station's recipe list opens.
3. A recipe becomes available when you have the ingredients in your inventory. You can craft several at once by choosing a quantity.
4. The result goes into your inventory. **If your inventory is full, the item drops on the ground** and you can pick it up again.

---

## Crafting stations

| Station | How to get it | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Hand** | Always available | Planks, Workbench, Torches |
| **Workbench** | Hand-craft: 4× Any Plank | Tools, weapons, armor, furniture, ammo, other stations |
| **Furnace** | Workbench: 6× Any Plank + 10× Any Stone | Smelting ore, refining stone |
| **Alchemy Table** | Workbench: 5× Diamond + 10× Any Stone + 6× Any Plank | Potions, Blood Compass, Dracula Pendant |

---

## Ingredient tags ("Any ...")

Some recipes ask for a **tag** instead of a specific item. Any item that carries that tag can be used.

| Tag | Accepted items (Demo + Full) | Full version only 🔒 |
|---|---|---|
| **Any Wood** | Wood | Mesquite Wood, Glacierore Wood |
| **Any Plank** | Plank | Mesquite Plank, Glacierore Plank |
| **Any Stone** | Stone | Stone Sand, Stone Snow |

Example: a recipe that needs `10× Any Stone` accepts, for instance, 4 Stone + 6 Stone Sand.

## Progression chain

```text
Chop tree ──► Wood ──(Hand)──► Plank ──(Hand)──► Workbench
                                                    │
        ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┤
        ▼                      ▼                    ▼
  Wood / Stone Pickaxe   Furnace (Plank+Stone)   Chest, Bed, Ladder, Walls...
        │                      │
        ▼                      ▼
  Coal, Iron Ore ──► Iron Ingot ──► Iron Pickaxe / Sword / Armor
        │
        ▼
  Gold Ore (Iron Pickaxe) ──► Gold Ingot ──► Gold set
        │
        ▼
  Diamond (Gold Pickaxe) ──► Diamond set + Alchemy Table ──► Dracula Pendant
```

## Tips

- The Stone → Smooth Stone recipe and the ore smelting recipes at the Furnace each use **1× Coal**. Stock up on Coal first.
- One Torch craft gives **10 torches** (1× Any Plank + 1× Coal).
- The Arrow recipe needs 1× Phoenix Feather and makes 10 arrows. Phoenix Feathers drop from Phoenixes, which appear at night. The Dart recipe doesn't need a feather.
- To craft the Dracula Pendant you must find a Pendant Fragment in **underground chests** (see [Mechanics](mechanics.md)).
