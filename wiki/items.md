# Items

This page lists the items in the game by category. For recipes see [Crafting](crafting.md); for tool tiers and the damage formula see [Mechanics](mechanics.md).

> 🔒 = Full version only.
> **"Obtained from"** column: *Craft* = made with a recipe (station in parentheses), *Chest* = found in world chests, *(enemy)* = dropped by an enemy, *Mined from the world* = broken out of the terrain.

### Pickaxes

Pickaxe speed and tier decide which blocks you can break and how fast.

| Pickaxe | Tier | Speed | Range | Obtained from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diamond Pickaxe | Diamond | 4.0 | 6 | Craft (Workbench) |
| Gold Pickaxe | Gold | 3.0 | 5 | Craft (Workbench) |
| Iron Pickaxe | Iron | 2.0 | 5 | Craft (Workbench) |
| Stone Pickaxe | Stone | 1.0 | 5 | Craft (Workbench) |
| Wooden Pickaxe | Wood | 0.75 | 5 | Craft (Workbench) |

Blocks you can break bare-handed: Dirt, Grass, Leaf, Wood (in the full version also Snow, Sand, and Mesquite / Glacierore Wood and Leaves). Hand breaking has speed **0.6** and range **6**.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Damage | Type | Obtained from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bow | 25 | Ranged / thrown — ammo: Arrow | Chest |
| Dart Launcher | 15 | Ranged / thrown — ammo: Dart | Craft (Workbench) |
| Diamond Sword | 20 (+ 15-damage thrown sword) | Melee | Craft (Workbench) |
| Gold Sword | 15 | Melee | Craft (Workbench) |
| Iron Sword | 10 | Melee | Craft (Workbench) |
| Pillow | 10 | Ranged / thrown | Chest, Creature (enemy) |
| Stone Sword | 7 | Melee | Craft (Workbench) |
| Wooden Sword | 5 | Melee | Craft (Workbench) |

- **Swords** are melee weapons with a range of 2 blocks and a use time of 0.2 s. The Diamond Sword also throws a sword-shaped projectile.
- The **Bow** consumes Arrows and the **Dart Launcher** consumes Darts. The ammo must be in your inventory.
- The **Pillow** uses no ammo; it is a short-range thrown weapon.
- The player's own base damage is 0; all damage comes from the weapon.

## Accessories

You have 3 accessory slots. Accessories apply their effect continuously while equipped.

| Accessory | Effect | Obtained from |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | Defense **+10** | Chest (2%) |
| **Sneakers** | Movement speed **×2** | Chest (2%) |
| **Jump Boots** | Jump force **×2** | Chest (2%) |
| **Four Leaf Clover** | Critical hit rate **+0.2** (+20 percentage points) | Chest (3%) |

The percentages come from the weights of the single roll in each chest's last ("rare") pool (see [Mechanics → Chests and loot](mechanics.md?id=chests-and-loot)).

> The Dracula Cape and Dracula Teeth look like accessories but currently have no effect and are **not usable in the demo**. They drop from Dracula.
