# Getting Started

Welcome to Deepbound! This page is for someone opening the game for the first time: how to start, what to do in the first hour, and which key does what.

---

## What is Deepbound?

Deepbound is a **2D adventure game** built around digging, crafting and survival in a block-based world. You chop trees, dive underground, mine ore and craft equipment; deep in the world, in the Castle Ruins, you summon **Dracula** and fight him. You can play alone or with a group of **up to 4 players**.

- **Developer:** Kankan Studios

- **Languages:** English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese

## Starting a game

1. Launch the game from Steam.
2. In the main menu choose **Singleplayer** → **New World**.
3. Enter a **World Name** (it only appears in your world list) and, optionally, a **Seed**. The same seed always creates the same world; leave it blank for a random world.
4. Press **Create**. Your character spawns on the surface, near the middle of the world.

### Multiplayer

- One player hosts the world through **Steam** or **IP** (Host World Steam / Host World IP).
- Others use **Join with Steam** or **Join with IP**.
- A world holds up to **4 players**.

## First steps: follow the Guide

The game leads you through your first hour with the **Guide**. Press `G` at any time to open it. There are **14 steps**, in this order:

| # | Step | What to do | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Chop Tree** | Left-click a tree and collect Wood. | No pickaxe needed; you can do it by hand. |
| 2 | **Craft Workbench** | Press `C`: 1 Wood → 4 Planks, then 4 Planks → Workbench. Place the Workbench. | See [Crafting](crafting.md) |
| 3 | **Craft Pickaxe** | Right-click the Workbench → Wooden Pickaxe (2 Wood + 5 Planks). | |
| 4 | **Mine Stone** | Head underground and mine Stone. | A Wooden Pickaxe can break Stone. |
| 5 | **Mine Coal** | Find Coal Ore. | Needs a Stone Pickaxe. Torches are made from Coal + Plank. |
| 6 | **Mine Iron** | Mine Iron Ore. | A Stone Pickaxe is enough. |
| 7 | **Smelt Iron** | Craft a Furnace (6 Planks + 10 Stone), then Raw Iron + Coal → Iron Ingot. | |
| 8 | **Craft Armor** | Craft iron armor. The Guide step needs the **Iron Chestplate**. | Needs 20 Iron Ingots. |
| 9 | **Mine Gold** | Mine Gold Ore. | Needs an **Iron Pickaxe**. |
| 10 | **Explore Chests** | Find and open a chest underground. | May contain a Pendant Fragment, a Compass, potions. |
| 11 | **Mine Diamond** | Mine Diamond Ore. | Needs a **Gold Pickaxe**. |
| 12 | **Craft Alchemy Table** | Craft an Alchemy Table at the Workbench. | Needs 5 Diamonds. |
| 13 | **Craft Dracula Pendant** | Craft the Pendant at the Alchemy Table. | Pendant Fragment + 3 Bat Wings + 2 Gold Ingots + Diamond |
| 14 | **Defeat Dracula** | Summon Dracula at the Vampire Altar in the Castle Ruins and defeat him. | Details: [Mechanics → Dracula](mechanics.md?id=dracula) |

After step 14, the demo shows the **"Demo Completed"** screen.

## Survival tips

- **Watch out at night!** Between 18:00 and 06:00 far more (and stronger) enemies spawn on the surface (Boars, Creatures, Phoenixes…). Build a bed and carry torches.
- **Make a bed and place it.** You respawn at your bed, and the **Return Mirror** teleports you to it.
- **Carry plenty of torches.** Caves are dark; a torch lights a radius of 10 blocks.
- **Chests are underground.** They can hold potions, arrows, torches and, rarely, accessories. Explore the underground.
- **Health regeneration starts after combat.** About 3.5 s after you stop fighting you regain 3 health per second (data: `RegenPerSecond: 3`, `CombatCooldown: 3.5`). Carry a **Healing Potion** for emergencies.
- **Dying is safe.** You keep all your items when you die; you just respawn after 10 seconds.
- **Dashing (Shift)** costs stamina (25). Use it to escape or to cross gaps.
- **Diamond and Gold are deep.** Go to the lowest 25% of the underground.
- **Autosave** runs every 5 minutes; still use *Save & Exit* when you quit.

For more, see [Mechanics](mechanics.md).
